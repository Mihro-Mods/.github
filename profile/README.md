Welcome to **Mihro Mods**. This organization is the central development studio where I adapt iconic fictional universes—across video games, animation, cinema, and anime—into **Terraria** expansions using **tModLoader (C# / .NET 8)**.

## Philosophy: Complete Universe Adaptations

Every project under **Mihro Mods** is engineered to bring the full identity of its universe into Terraria rather than isolated item packs:

* **Strict Modularity & Zero Cross-Contamination:** Content is deployed in sequenced expansions (e.g., *Marvel's Avengers #1*, *Star Wars' Galactic Civil War #1*). No items, NPCs, or pets appear where they don't belong in the lore.
* **Horizontal Scaling (Tier Systems I-IV):** Progression is open. You don't have to wait until Moon Lord to use your favorite lightsaber or Marvel suit; gear is acquired early and upgraded at custom workbenches across the entire playthrough.
* **Signature Combat Overhauls:** Deep gameplay systems replacing standard Terraria loops—from lightsaber parrying and Castlevania's Item Crashes to psychological horror events.
* **Immersive Economies:** Deterministic, lore-friendly acquisition. Forge Beskar through bounty hunting, or unlock Slasher vanities by collecting VHS tapes and Double Feature Tickets.
