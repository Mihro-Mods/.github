# Mihro Mods

Welcome to Mihro Mods. This organization is the development hub where I adapt mechanics, items, and combat systems from iconic franchises into the Terraria engine using **tModLoader (C# / .NET)**.

## Project Roadmap

*   🟢 **Terraria's Hollow Knight** *(In Development)* - Adaptation of the Charms system and pets from the Hollow Knight universe.
*   ⚪ **Terraria's DOOM** *(Planned)* - Integration of heavy arsenal and aggressive close-quarters combat mechanics.
*   ⚪ **Terraria's Castlevania** *(Planned)* - Expansion of the Summoner class with classic weapons and cross magic.
*   ⚪ **Terraria's Star Wars** *(Planned)* - Lightsabers, Force equipment, Planets and NPC.
